## One design change from what I found

Gemini's own transcription is *better* than HPR's Whisper text (it got names right that Whisper mangled). But LLM-generated timestamps drift, and timestamps are this product's entire spine — click-to-seek, scroll-sync, and every Ask citation resolve to them. A drifting timestamp is a broken product.

So: **HPR's Whisper segments stay as the timestamp spine, and Gemini's job is narrowed to attaching speakers to them.** Machine-accurate timing, model-quality attribution. It also makes the output tiny — just segment-id → speaker — so no output-cap risk and much faster passes.
