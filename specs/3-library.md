# 3 — Library

## Goal

Everything around the call page: the home screen, the meetings list, and finding a call among many.
Third, not first — a beautiful list of calls you cannot open is worth nothing.

## Contract

- Home: calls grouped by day, each card showing title, duration, waveform art and a one-line
  AI summary, matching the real product's layout.
- Navigation shell: My Calls / Team Calls / Playlists / Alerts, with unbuilt sections honestly
  marked rather than dead-ending.
- **Search across calls** — matches inside transcripts, not just titles, and lands you on the
  matching moment.
- Empty and loading states that look deliberate.
